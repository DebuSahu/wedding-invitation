## ✨ Features & Mobile Innovations

- **Mobile-First Luxury App Shell:** Native smartphone experience with smooth scrolling, high-contrast readable typography (`Playfair Display`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, and `Rozha One`), and zero horizontal overflow.
- **Synthesized Shehnai & Tanpura Engine:** Pure Web Audio API traditional Raag Yaman Shehnai performance with no audio buffer delays, auto-unlocked cleanly on mobile tap/touch.
- **Live Shubh Muhurat Countdown:** Real-time countdown to 2nd February 2027 with direct one-tap Google Calendar and Apple / iCal file downloads.
- **Shubh Karyakram Itinerary:** Elegant schedule cards for Mehendi, Haldi, Royal Sangeet (exclusive Black Tie theme), Shubh Vivah, and Grand Reception.
- **Interactive Saptapadi (Saat Phere):** Interactive 7 Vedic vows tab carousel with Sanskrit shlokas and English translations.
- **Interactive Diya Lighting:** Visitors can light a virtual prayer diya accompanied by a resonant temple brass chime.
- **RSVP & Wall of Wishes:** Guests can confirm attendance and post heartfelt blessings to the guestbook stored in local storage.
- **Instant WhatsApp Sharing:** One-tap button formatted with personalized wedding invitation details.
- **Direct Venue Maps:** Google Maps routing to Gulmohar Guest House (Prayagraj) and Hotel Surya Galaxy (Shuklaganj, Unnao).
