## 🌟 Key Highlights & Features

1. **Grand Royal Wax Seal & 3D Gate Opening Ceremony**
   - Traditional palace doors with antique gold jaali arch patterns.
   - Lord Ganesha embossed wax seal (`शुभ विवाह`).
   - Clicking *"Open Royal Invitation"* swings the gates open in 3D perspective, showers celebratory rose petals, and begins traditional Shehnai music.

2. **Auspicious Sanskrit Invocations**
   - Authentic Devanagari Sanskrit shloka with poetic English translation:
     *॥ ॐ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥ वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय सूर्यकोटि समप्रभ...*
   - Sacred Kalash, Swastik, Mayur (Peacock), and Airavata (Baraat Elephant) vector motifs.

3. **Background Instrument: Traditional Shehnai & Tanpura (Web Audio API)**
   - Synthesizes authentic Indian classical Shehnai melodies (*Raag Yaman & Festive Mangal Dhun*) and resonant wood-bodied Tanpura drone.
   - **Zero external audio dependencies**—guaranteed to play seamlessly offline without 404 or CORS errors.
   - Animated soundwave bars with live play/pause controls.
   - Sacred bronze temple bell chime (*Ghanti*) when lighting the virtual Diya!

4. **Real-time Flower Petals & Gold Dust Canvas Engine**
   - Natural aerodynamic fluttering physics with 3D petal flipping and wind sway.
   - 4 Interactive modes toggled directly from the top bar:
     - 🌸 **Rose Petal Shower** (*Gulab Patti*)
     - 🌼 **Marigold Shower** (*Genda Phool*)
     - ✨ **Gold Dust Shimmer**
     - 🚫 **Petals Pause**

5. **Shubh Muhurat Live Countdown & Calendar Integration**
   - Live Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds countdown to **2nd February 2027**.
   - One-click **"Add to Google Calendar"** & **"Download Apple / Outlook (.ics)"** file generator.

6. **The Couple's Story: *Do Dil Ek Dastan***
   - Groom (*Divyanshu Sahu*) & Bride (*Sapna Kushwaha*) portraits in gilded jharokha frames.
   - Personal bio, heartfelt vows, and *"How Destiny Wove Our Path"* romantic milestone timeline.

7. **Shubh Karyakram (Complete Wedding Itinerary)**
   - **Ganesh Pujan & Mehendi**: Sunday, 31st January 2027 (Prayagraj)
   - **Mangal Haldi & Phoolon Ki Holi**: Monday, 1st February 2027 (Prayagraj)
   - **Royal Sangeet Gala**: Monday, 1st February 2027 • Evening — **Dress Code: Royal Black**
   - **Shubh Vivaha (Baraat & Pheras)**: Tuesday, 2nd February 2027 • **Gulmohar Guest House, Prayagraj**
   - **The Grand Royal Reception**: Friday, 5th February 2027 • **Hotel Surya Galaxy, Shuklaganj, Unnao**

8. **Saptapadi: The Sacred Seven Vows (Saat Phere)**
   - Interactive sacred fire (*Agni Kund*) with flickering holy flames.
   - 7 interactive step discs revealing the ancient Vedic Sanskrit shlokas and translations.

9. **Interactive "Light a Diya for the Couple" Ritual**
   - Guests can touch the traditional brass Diya or click the blessing button to light a virtual lamp.
   - Rings an auspicious temple bell, bursts flower petals, and increments the live global blessing counter.

10. **RSVP & Royal Blessings Book (Live Guestbook)**
    - Guest RSVP confirmation (attendance status, number of guests, city, message).
    - Stored in browser `localStorage` and displayed on the *Royal Wall of Warm Wishes*.

11. **Instant WhatsApp Share & Print E-Invite (PDF)**
    - Floating action buttons on mobile and desktop.
    - Pre-filled WhatsApp invitation text with direct link.
    - Clean print stylesheet (`@media print`) that formats the card into an elegant physical keepsake PDF.

## 🚀 How to Run & View

### Local Preview:
1. Double-click `index.html` to open directly in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox), OR
2. Run a local server:
   ```bash
   python -m http.server 8080
   ```
   and visit `http://localhost:8080`.
